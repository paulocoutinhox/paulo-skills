# Social Thumbnail

Create a polished thumbnail that accurately represents the underlying content and remains understandable at a small display size. Read [design and marketing guidance](references/design.md) and [platform export profiles](references/platforms.md).

## Intake

Ask for unresolved essentials: content/topic, audience, platform/surface, exact text and language, visual style, brand references, aspect ratio, required output format, pixel dimensions, upload device, and byte limit. Default to 16:9 when no format is chosen. Offer a large PNG master and a JPEG derivative starting at quality 99 under 2,000,000 bytes as the owner's portable preset, while explaining the current platform-specific limits.

## Workflow

1. Establish the real content promise. Select a clear focal subject, a short readable text hierarchy, and a theme-appropriate palette. Use [the creative brief](assets/thumbnail-brief.md).
2. Research current relevant creator examples and official guidance. Treat examples as inspiration, not assets to copy or a formula for guaranteed clicks.
3. Generate original artwork using the available image model at the largest useful native resolution and the selected composition. If the model lacks the exact aspect ratio, plan a deliberate crop or extension. Never stretch proportions to fit.
4. Add or refine exact text using an appropriate readable licensed font. A model may generate lettering, but inspect every glyph. Use deterministic editable typography when needed for spelling, alignment, or brand precision.
5. Inspect the image at full size and around 320 pixels wide. Check text, spelling, contrast, subject proportions, faces, hands, geometry, alignment, crop safety, and platform overlays. Repair defects before export.
6. Preserve the large PNG master. Use [the export utility](references/export.md) for an explicitly selected JPEG size, quality, fit method, and byte ceiling. Do not silently lower quality, crop, resize, or replace the master when an export fails.
7. Reopen the actual final file, verify dimensions, format, bytes, color handling, and readability. Deliver the master, requested derivatives, brief, and export settings. Do not call an uninspected image finished.

When variants are requested, change a meaningful concept, such as focal subject or headline framing. Use a platform's supported test only when separately authorized. Content creation does not authorize publication. Consult [sources](references/sources.md) for current limits and evidence.
